📊 Sales and Profit Performance Analysis
📌 Project Description
  The Sales and Profit Performance Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales, profit, customer behavior, regional performance, and product categories. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, and make data-driven decisions through clear visualizations and KPIs.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, quantity sold, and total orders.

- Compare sales performance across different regions.

- Evaluate category-wise sales and profitability.

- Understand customer segment and customer type distribution.

- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.

- Support business decision-making using interactive dashboards.

📌 Business Questions (KPIs)

1) What is the total sales generated?

2) What is the total profit earned?

3) How many products were sold (Quantity)?

4) many customer orders were received?

5) Which region generated the highest sales?

6) Which product category contributed the highest sales and profit?

7) How does sales and profit change month by month?

8) Which customer segment contributes the most sales?

9) Which customer type generates the highest profit?

📈 Project Insights

- The business generated approximately 54M in Total Sales.

- Overall Profit reached around 11M, indicating healthy business performance.

- A total of 23K products were sold across 4,265 orders.

- East region recorded the highest sales, while North showed comparatively lower sales.

- Office Supplies, Furniture, and Technology categories all contributed significantly, with Office Supplies showing a slight lead in sales.

- Monthly sales remained relatively stable, while profit showed noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.

- Customer sales are distributed among Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments, showing a balanced customer base.

- Customer types such as Regular, Premium, New, and Returning contributed almost equally to overall profit.

✅ Final Conclusion
  The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance by combining sales, profit, customer, regional, and category analysis into a single interactive report. It helps decision-makers quickly identify high-performing regions, profitable product categories, customer trends, and monthly performance. These insights can be used to improve sales strategies, optimize business operations, and drive sustainable business growth.
